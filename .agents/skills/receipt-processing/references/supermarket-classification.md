# Supermarket classification

Apply these four categories only to supermarket receipts. Reuse matching private registry
IDs and descriptions. Animal supply, vet, fuel and discount-retailer categories keep their
existing definitions.

Distinguish explicit cat products (cat food, treats, litter), plausible shelter supplies
(kitchen rolls, trash bags, cleaning supplies, plain meat), and clearly personal items.
Inspect all retained receipt pages. Merchant name or a varied basket alone is insufficient.
Do not invent contents or purpose from an unreadable product label.

| Category                             | Whole-receipt rule                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Supermarket - clear cat shelter use  | At least one explicit cat product; every other item can plausibly serve the shelter. No clearly personal items.   |
| Supermarket - mixed, clear cat       | Explicit cat products plus clearly personal items; plausible shelter supplies may also be present.                |
| Supermarket - mixed, likely personal | No explicit cat products, but at least one plausible shelter supply, with or without ordinary personal groceries. |
| Supermarket - personal               | Neither explicit cat products nor plausible shelter supplies.                                                     |

A basket of only plausible supplies belongs in likely personal: without an explicit cat
product, do not assert clear cat shelter use. These sorting labels do not establish actual
ownership, reimbursement eligibility or bank allocation.

Use existing `evidence` notes: **Category:** names the actual supporting items. Cat treats
plus tomatoes support mixed, clear cat; trash bags plus cheese support mixed, likely
personal; cat food plus kitchen rolls support clear cat shelter use. Identify ambiguous
labels rather than treating all meat or household goods as explicit cat products.

When certainty is below high, add **Confidence:** naming the uncertain fields and why the
image, grouping or OCR evidence leaves them uncertain. Update after reassessment without
rewriting the initial notes. High confidence does not certify deferred financial fields;
retain the existing deferred-finance note.
